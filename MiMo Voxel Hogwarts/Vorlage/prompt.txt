A single webpage intricately rendered 3d voxel scene of a detailed voxel-art diorama of Hogwarts from Harry Potter, rendered as if built from tiny, perfectly aligned cubic blocks, viewed in high-angle isometric projection on a clean pastel sky-blue background. The building sits on a massive, multi-tiered floating circular chunk of grassy soil and dark stone with sharp cuboid edges. All features of Hogwarts and it's surroundings are visible and masterfully crafted from high grain voxel blocks matching the colors of the detail they build. Soft midday lighting with crisp, short diagonal shadows, clean cube edges, no anti-aliasing, Harry Potter color palette. The camera can freely move through the scene with wasd keys or the mouse, the camera can zoom in and out using the keyboard or the mouse wheel.
