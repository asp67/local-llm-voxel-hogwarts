A single webpage intricately rendered 3d voxel scene of a detailed voxel-art diorama of Hogwarts from Harry Potter, rendered as if built from tiny, perfectly aligned cubic blocks, viewed in high-angle isometric projection on a clean dar-blue night sky background. The building sits on a massive, multi-tiered floating circular chunk of ground with sharp cuboid edges. All features of Hogwarts and it's surroundings are visible and masterfully crafted from high grain voxel blocks matching the colors of the detail they build. Dark gloomy midnight lighting with soft, short diagonal shadows, clean cube edges, no anti-aliasing, Harry Potter color palette. The camera can freely move through the scene with wasd keys or the mouse, the camera can zoom in and out using the keyboard or the mouse wheel.
The images are examples of the way the user visualizes the result.
